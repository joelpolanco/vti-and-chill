How it works

  1. Parse — your CSV is read in your browser. We auto-detect Robinhood, Vanguard, Fidelity, and Schwab column layouts, and fall back to a manual mapper for anything else.
  2. Fetch prices — for each unique symbol (and VTI), we pull historical daily closes through a price API. Prices are split-adjusted.
  3. Replay — for every buy of $X on day D, we credit you with X / VTI_close(D) shares of VTI. Sells release shares proportionally. Your VTI sleeve is then valued at today's close.
  4. Compare — we compute current portfolio value, what VTI would be worth, and the dollar and percentage alpha.
·

Notes & caveats

  • This tool uses split-adjusted closing prices. Your actual fills may differ by intraday timing.
  • Dividends are reflected via adjusted close, so reinvested dividends are already baked into both your symbols and VTI.
  • For a buy on a non-trading day (weekend / holiday), we use the next available trading day's close.
  • Options, crypto, mutual funds without ticker symbols, and non-USD-listed securities aren't supported in this version.
  • Your data stays in your browser. CSVs are parsed locally. The only thing sent to our server is the list of symbol names so we can fetch their price history.
