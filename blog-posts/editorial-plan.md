# VTI & Chill — 50 Blog Posts Editorial Plan

## Brand Voice
- Confident but approachable, slightly irreverent, anti-Wall Street, pro-simplicity
- Tone: Like a smart friend who happens to know finance — not a stuffy advisor
- Use "you" language, occasional humor, pop culture references
- Always end with legal disclaimer
